Digital Twin Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Digital Twin is a personal automation service operated for one configured owner. It collects requested records from connected services, produces private analytics and publishes selected results to the owner's Sofia Planner deployment.

Google data accessed

With the owner's authorization, Digital Twin may access Gmail messages and attachments, Calendar events, Drive files, Tasks and Fit activity and health observations. It accesses only the data needed for the capture, planning, reporting and automation features the owner enables.

How Google data is used

Google data is used to capture requested records, maintain the owner's task and calendar context, calculate personal health and planning analytics and prepare private reports. When the owner enables the relevant automation, Digital Twin may archive or label messages, prepare or send owner-directed email, create or update Drive files, create or respond to Calendar events and create, update, move, complete or delete Tasks. Google data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Storage and processing

Original records and derived results may be retained in the owner's private local data lake and private Redis-backed resource store. Sofia Planner may also keep account-scoped interface preferences and unsaved planning drafts in the owner's browser storage. Configured service providers may process data only when needed to provide the requested automation features.

Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate the configured features, when the owner directs a transfer, or when required by law. Access to private planner resources is restricted to the configured owner.

Retention and control

Data is retained for the owner's configured planning and automation history. The owner can revoke Google access from their Google Account and can delete retained records from the connected services, private data lake, Redis store and browser storage.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Digital Twin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Privacy questions can be raised through the Sofia Planner project issue tracker.
